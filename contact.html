Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about appointments, insurance, and visits.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Call us at +91 98765 43210 or use the contact form above. You can also book online through our patient portal.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we work with most major vision and medical insurance providers. Please contact us to verify your specific plan.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a valid ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any previous eye records if available.

Is emergency eye care available?

Yes, our emergency eye care line is available 24/7. Call our main number and follow the prompts for urgent assistance.
