Comprehensive Eye Exam

A thorough assessment of your vision and eye health using digital refraction, tonometry, and retinal imaging. We detect issues early — from refractive errors to glaucoma — before they affect your daily life.

  • Digital visual acuity & refraction testing
  • Eye pressure (glaucoma) screening
  • Dilated retinal health inspection

Cataract Surgery

Restore clear vision with bladeless, laser-assisted cataract surgery. Our surgeons use premium intraocular lenses (IOLs) tailored to your lifestyle — whether you want sharp distance vision or reading clarity.

  • Femtosecond laser technology
  • Premium monofocal & multifocal IOLs
  • Same-day discharge, rapid recovery

Service 03

LASIK Vision Correction

Say goodbye to glasses and contacts. Our bladeless LASIK procedure reshapes the cornea with precision laser technology, correcting nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism — often in under 20 minutes.

  • Bladeless, all-laser procedure
  • Custom wavefront-guided treatment
  • Most patients return to work next day

Service 04

Pediatric Eye Care

Gentle, child-friendly exams that make kids feel at ease. We screen for lazy eye, crossed eyes, and early refractive errors — and build treatment plans that support healthy visual development.

  • Amblyopia (lazy eye) treatment
  • Strabismus (eye alignment) care
  • Myopia control & vision therapy

Service 05

Retina & Glaucoma Care

Advanced diagnostics and treatment for sight-threatening conditions like diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, and glaucoma. Our retina specialists use OCT imaging and targeted therapies to preserve vision.

  • OCT retinal imaging & monitoring
  • Intravitreal injections & laser therapy
  • Glaucoma pressure management
